Rates used

UK income tax and NI thresholds are confirmed as unchanged for 2027/28. Scottish bands for 2027/28, Child Benefit and student loan thresholds were not set when this was built, so the 2026/27 figures are used. Tax is worked out month by month on the cumulative basis payroll uses. The allowance is reduced when income is over £100,000. Higher-rate relief on after-tax pension contributions, which you claim from HMRC yourself, is not included. This is an estimate, not financial or tax advice. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.
